# Reference verification

Verify that every reference exists before accepting it. Never guess a DOI, a year, a volume, an issue, or page numbers. Never invent title, author, year, ISSN, DOI, volume, issue, pages, publisher, or access date. When a metadata field is absent, omit it only when the citation style allows it. Otherwise mark it `[metadata needs verification]`.

## Where to check

- Indonesian journals: Garuda and SINTA. Search by title and author. Record the journal name, ISSN, volume, issue, and pages as listed there.
- International sources: OpenAlex and Crossref. Search by title or DOI. A DOI must resolve. A DOI that fails to resolve is marked UNVERIFIED.
- Books: publisher catalog or library catalog. Record author, year, title, edition when relevant, city, and publisher.
- Theses and dissertations: author, year, title, degree type, city, and institution.
- Proceedings: authors, year, title, proceedings or workshop title, location, volume, and pages.
- Standards and patents: issuing body, year, number, and title.
- Reports: author or editor, year, title, organization, and report number.
- Internet sources: official URL or site only. Record author, upload year, title, full URL or site name, and access date (day, month, year). Blogspot, Wordpress, and Wikipedia are excluded.

## Citation audit

- Every in-text citation must have a matching bibliography entry.
- Every bibliography entry must be cited in the text at least once.
- Report any citation without an entry and any entry without a citation as a finding. Never silently fix it by inventing metadata.
- Check the journal entry schema: authors, year, title, journal full name or abbreviation, volume and issue, pages.
- Check author names: for three or more authors, first author plus et al. or dkk. For multiword names, use the surname. Institutional authors may use a concise official acronym.
- Check direct quotes: quoted text must reproduce the source exactly, including wording, spelling, and punctuation. Short quotes under 5 lines are double-spaced, integrated, and quoted. Long quotes of 5 lines or more are single-spaced, indented, and unquoted.
- When the guide text labels a long-quote rule in a way that contradicts its own example, quote the guide exact language and flag the inconsistency for supervisor confirmation. Never silently correct it.

## Age-window ambiguity

Guides differ on reference age. Some guides ask for literature from the 5 years before the research starts, for the literature review and related research. Some guides ask for references within the last 10 years for the bibliography. Treat these as separate guide statements. When the guide the user follows is unclear on a category, report both statements and recommend supervisor clarification. Never silently pick one window.
